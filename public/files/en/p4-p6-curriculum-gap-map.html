P4-P6 curriculum coverage workbook

BEETS AI · Theme A · Resource #2 · v0.1 (draft) · School planning · P4-P6

⚠️ All examples on this page are fictional and do not refer to any real school or person. This file is a draft and must be reviewed by an educator before publication.

How to use

  1. In the blank coverage table, record each subject's existing AI-literacy activities by subject and term.
  2. Check them against the P4-P6 stage goals below and mark covered, repeated and empty.
  3. In the gap-and-next-step table, write the smallest next step and an owner for each gap.
  4. Use the meeting checklist to confirm the map leads to a next step, then turn the page to compare with the three fictional examples.

P4-P6 stage goals (suggested list)

Note: The goal rows below are this workbook's suggested scaffold, not the framework's own wording. Check them against the P4-P6 stage goals in F (the AI Literacy Learning Framework for Primary and Secondary Schools, pp.1-6 to 1-10) before you quote them.

G1Understand what AI is and where it appears in everyday life
G2Use AI safely and responsibly, and know what information should not be entered or shared

G6Understand data and personal privacy, and know how information is collected and used

Meeting checklist

□ Existing activities state "which subject, which level, what was taught"
□ Every goal is marked "covered / repeated / empty"
□ Every gap has one smallest next step
□ Every next step has an owner (a named person or post)
□ Each next step is small enough to finish within one term
□ Each activity can be tied to the EDB framework (F pp.1-6 to 1-10 or B p.2)

How this relates to the EDB framework

Maps to F (the AI Literacy Learning Framework for Primary and Secondary Schools, pp.1-6 to 1-10): cross-subject implementation and progression across four learning stages, used to check a school's own activities against the stage goals; and B (the Blueprint for Digital Education Development in Primary and Secondary Schools (three-fold leaflet), p.2): digital and AI education strategies in school development plans with a clear implementation timetable.
