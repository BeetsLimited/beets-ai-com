An AI wrote this

AI-generated (for demonstration)

"According to the data, Hong Kong's population rose steadily from about 5.6 million in 1991 to 7,413,070 in 2021, and has kept rising every year since. It is projected to reach 9 million by 2030."

Your task: do not simply trust it. Check each claim against the official data, then draw your own correct line graph.

Verification steps

  1. Does the passage state where its data came from?
  2. Find each figure it gives and compare it with the official data.
  3. Check whether its description of the trend ("rises every year") is supported by the data.
  4. Check whether its claim about the future ("projected 9 million") has any basis.

Answer key

ItemKey point
No sourceThe whole passage never says where its figures came from — this is the first warning sign. Pupils should learn to ask the AI to cite its source.
The 2021 figureCheck it. This figure matches the official data — it is used to show that "the AI can also be right", so truth cannot be judged from tone alone.
"rises every year"Does not match the data. After checking the official data you will find years when it did not rise. This is the most important finding of the lesson.
"projected 9 million"Has no basis, and confuses "facts that have happened" with "predictions". Pupils should point out that it gives no method for the projection.
The 1991 figureThe AI gives no source; check it against the official data before deciding.
